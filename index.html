Version 5

Turn your photos into a festival map.

Everything runs in your browser. Photos are never uploaded. Only map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap.

Important: if you pick photos through your phone's Photos / Gallery picker, the browser usually receives them with GPS removed (Android and iOS both do this for privacy). Google Photos "location" may also exist only in Google's database, not in the file. Use "Choose files (keeps GPS)" below and select the original files from a folder (e.g. Downloads, DCIM/Camera), or downloaded originals from Google Photos.
